AE company outside Odoo's applicable Peppol transport must not update its partner's
Peppol verification state merely to open the invoice review wizard. Native applicable
Peppol and other countries retain their endpoint verification.

an ASP/FTA acknowledgement or grant contact write access to accounting operators.
