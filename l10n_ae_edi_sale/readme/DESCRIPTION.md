Optional Sales integration for the UAE PINT-AE localization. Preserve native Sales
down payment calculations and add the original taxed advance's number, date and
consumed net/VAT to the frozen final invoice evidence and XML references. The base
accounting localization remains installable without Sales.

The current checkpoint covers one AED taxed advance and its final balance, with
automated checks for unposted advances, another commercial customer and an excessive
deduction. Multiple advances, cumulative/concurrent consumption, the complete fiscal
matrix and browser acceptance remain under development.
