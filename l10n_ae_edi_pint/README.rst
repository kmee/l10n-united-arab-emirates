Extend the public Community UBL/PINT generator for UAE identifiers and
document processes. Freeze the generated XML at preparation and validate
it using pinned UBL XSD plus shared and UAE Schematron rules. Reviewed
incoming AED billing and self-billing documents use the public UBL21
importer with existing counterparties and taxes, protected original
evidence and economic accounting roles. Additional scenario fields,
foreign currency and the full conformance matrix remain under
development. This module does not submit directly to Peppol or the FTA.

Usage
=====

Install only in an isolated Odoo 20.0 development database. Read the
repository architecture and QA instructions before preparing synthetic
documents. These Alpha modules are not approved for production fiscal
transmission.

Accounting managers can open **Received UAE Documents**, inspect the
preserved XML and select **Process Received Document**. A supported
ordinary AED tax invoice creates a supplier bill in draft; **Open
Accounting Document** opens it for accounting review. Unknown
suppliers/taxes, invalid XML and unsupported scenarios remain in review
with diagnostics. The original evidence cannot be edited, and a received
document cannot be emitted again.

This inbox currently receives from the separately configured KMEE
loopback mock worker. It does not connect to an accredited provider
sandbox or constitute FTA acceptance.

Received tax credit notes in AED can become draft supplier refunds when
their single preceding invoice reference matches an existing supplier
bill. The declared credit reason, prepaid amount and payable amount
remain available for review. Processing does not post, reconcile or
import payments. Missing or ambiguous references stay in the
received-document review inbox with their original XML.

Ordinary AED commercial invoices and credits can also be processed. A
supplier without a VAT identifier must already have the matching routing
endpoint, legal registration number/type and issuing authority. Unknown
identities and tax mappings stay in review. The received category and
original currency are protected; declared amounts keep the XML currency
even if the accounting draft is reviewed in another currency.

Buyer-issued self-billed invoices and credits in AED can become draft
customer invoices and refunds when the local company is the XML
supplier. A manager must maintain one valid UAE agreement with local
role **Supplier Receiving Self-Billing** for the known buyer. Existing
matching billing drafts are reused without replacing their lines or
origin identifiers; differences remain in review. A received self-billed
credit needs one matching original self-billed sale. The agreement
reference at receipt remains available after later agreement changes.
Posting received sales never emits them again.

When the sender declares a prepaid amount, the accounting history
identifies it as a remote declaration for review. Receiving does not
create a local payment. Confirm any local settlement through the usual
accounting review and posting process.

The repository `scenario based user
manual <https://github.com/kmee/l10n-eua/blob/20.0-add-uae-edi/docs/user/scenarios-pt_BR.md>`__
distinguishes tested user journeys from workflows still to implement.

For foreign-currency emission, enter the reviewed AED-per-document-unit
quote, its date and source in **UAE E-Invoicing** before checking or
posting. The quote must be positive, use a decimal point and have at
most six decimal places. The frozen evidence preserves it and the
converted AED line/header values. A mismatch against an AED ledger
blocks preparation for accounting review. The rate source/date are human
references; automatic CBUAE acquisition and full tax-point/rounding
policy remain pending.

For a received foreign-currency bill, review **Amounts Declared by the
Sender** in the UAE tab. Document currency, declared exchange rate,
fiscal AED VAT and gross amounts remain protected. They do not set the
local reviewed quote or create a payment. Currency must already be
active; inconsistent fiscal values stay in the inbox for review. Worked
USD invoice/partial-credit vectors are tested; full combinations remain
pending.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

Complete the accepted specification in the KMEE brain repository,
including all UAE transaction scenarios, foreign currency, full fiscal
identity and ingestion, multiple credit references and advances, billing
reconciliation, operational receiving, migration and release readiness.
Current offline validation and controlled receiving checkpoints provide
partial evidence; they do not complete the full acceptance catalogue or
accredited ASP homologation.
