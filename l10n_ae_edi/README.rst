Validate confirmed company TINs and Free Zone beneficiary presence.
Opt-in company policy creates a frozen accounting snapshot and
transmission in the native posting transaction. A separate controlled
worker validates PINT, persists attempts and sends to a loopback mock
after commit. Authenticated controlled events preserve independent
validation, delivery and tax-report evidence. Production preparation is
disabled; the mock contract is KMEE's own.

Usage
=====

Install only in an isolated Odoo 20.0 development database. Read the
architecture and QA instructions before preparing synthetic documents.
In the company's UAE E-Invoicing tab, confirm identity, select a sandbox
ASP binding and optionally enable queuing on posting. An explicit
invoice binding overrides the company default. The controlled external
process sends committed intents; API acceptance does not confirm
delivery or FTA reporting. These Alpha modules are not approved for
production fiscal transmission.

For an outbound self-billed purchase, use a native self-billing purchase
journal and select a UAE Self-Billing Agreement in the invoice's UAE
tab. Accounting managers open **Invoicing → Configuration → UAE
Self-Billing Agreements** to maintain buyer-role agreements for the
company and commercial supplier, including reference, supply scope,
validity dates and a reference to reviewed supplier onboarding evidence.
Both parties need UAE VAT TRNs and eligible participant endpoints.
Missing or expired agreements block automatic posting/queuing. Only tax
invoices/credit notes are eligible; ordinary purchases and received
documents do not emit. Invoice389 and credit261 preserve the company as
buyer issuer, the partner as supplier and the selected agreement in
immutable transmission evidence. The checks do not query a live VAT or
ASP registry.

For incoming buyer-issued self-billing, use a separate agreement with
local role **Supplier Receiving Self-Billing**, referencing the known
buyer and reviewed onboarding evidence. Received accounting forms
display the original agreement reference and process without enabling
another outbound transmission.

For prerequisites, UI steps, expected results and error handling, read
the repository `scenario based user
manual <https://github.com/kmee/l10n-eua/blob/20.0-add-uae-edi/docs/user/scenarios-pt_BR.md>`__.
