UAE Electronic Invoicing

Validate confirmed company TINs and Free Zone beneficiary presence. Opt-in company policy creates a frozen accounting snapshot and transmission in the native posting transaction. A separate controlled worker validates PINT, persists attempts and sends to a loopback mock after commit. Authenticated controlled events preserve independent validation, delivery and tax-report evidence. Production preparation is disabled; the mock contract is KMEE’s own.

Authenticated fiscal evidence packages include reviewed retention obligations and the complete hold/release history in a versioned appendix. Native read and Data Export rights apply to every linked row; restricted history refuses export. Classification does not change the original fiscal bytes or authorize deletion.

Reviewed document deadlines retain an explicit rule, event, calendar, dates and owner. Native read-only assessments distinguish API receipts from complete confirmations. Internal reminders use the current UAE date, native activities and a bounded scheduled action; duplicate requests preserve one reminder. A record requiring review does not block other eligible reminders. No legal date is inferred from the invoice date and no internal reminder dispatches or reports a fiscal document.

Important

This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning. Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production. More details on development status

Usage

Install only in an isolated Odoo 20.0 development database. In the company’s UAE E-Invoicing tab, confirm identity, select a sandbox ASP binding and optionally enable queuing on posting. An explicit invoice binding overrides the company default. The controlled external process sends committed intents; API acceptance does not confirm delivery or FTA reporting. These Alpha modules are not approved for production fiscal transmission.

For a separate billing query account, assign UAE Integration → Billing Status Reader to a plain active internal user with only its permitted companies. The native Odoo20 JSON2 API provides scoped status, preserved PINT XML and paginated event facts without accounting or sending privileges. Preparing an existing posted sandbox invoice is a separate operation requiring native Invoice/write permissions. Native API keys belong to Odoo; the addon adds no key store or credential fields. Full client creation/callback integration remains pending.

Fiscal Evidence Auditor adds read access to transport attempts, original billing receipts, local correction history and received documents. It inherits Billing Status Reader and grants no accounting, provider configuration, dispatch or processing rights. Native Data Export must be granted separately; internal worker tokens stay restricted. Open UAE Audit to review issued/received documents, attempts, confirmations, billing receipts and corrections in read-only views. Original PINT XML downloads the preserved issue bytes after checking their hash; it creates no attachment or send. For a permitted export, select the issue and use native Actions → Export. Company and role revocation apply to subsequent reads, exports and downloads. Full archive/retention and customer security acceptance remain required.

For an outbound self-billed purchase, use a native self-billing purchase journal and select a UAE Self-Billing Agreement in the invoice’s UAE tab. Accounting managers open Invoicing → Configuration → UAE Self-Billing Agreements to maintain buyer-role agreements for the company and commercial supplier, including reference, supply scope, validity dates and a reference to reviewed supplier onboarding evidence. Both parties need UAE VAT TRNs and eligible participant endpoints. Missing or expired agreements block automatic posting/queuing. Only tax invoices/credit notes are eligible; ordinary purchases and received documents do not emit. Invoice389 and credit261 preserve the company as buyer issuer, the partner as supplier and the selected agreement in immutable transmission evidence. The checks do not query a live VAT or ASP registry.

For incoming buyer-issued self-billing, use a separate agreement with local role Supplier Receiving Self-Billing, referencing the known buyer and reviewed onboarding evidence. Received accounting forms display the original agreement reference and process without enabling another outbound transmission.

For an outgoing UAE sale adjustment, select the official UAE Credit Note Reason in the native Credit Note wizard, then use Reverse to create a draft. Review any partial quantities/prices and original reference before checking and posting. The code is also editable in the draft’s UAE tab. Free-text reason alone does not satisfy the emission check; automatic preparation blocks posting until an official code is selected. Received documents and ordinary vendor reversals do not use this outbound wizard field.

For a buyer-issued self-billed purchase adjustment, use the same native wizard and review partial lines. The resulting vendor credit retains the self-billing journal, official reason and original reference. Select an eligible UAE Self-Billing Agreement again for its company, commercial supplier and date: the wizard does not copy it. Missing agreement blocks Check and Prepare before UUID/transmission. With automatic preparation enabled, native Post retains posted accounting and records Requires Review. Supplier contacts use their commercial entity’s agreements. Confirm the purchase fiscal position and line VAT with the tax specialist if the expected tax is unavailable.

For a reviewed commercial sale or credit, select Commercial Document and the taxes determined by the tax specialist. With the PINT addon installed, IBR-122-AE blocks standard VAT at Check UAE Data and Prepare UAE E-Invoice. Automatic preparation on Confirm retains posted accounting and records Requires Review, without a UUID/transmission. Review the line taxes; the system does not change their classification automatically. Formal PINT validation remains required. The native credit wizard preserves the commercial category, which is protected after preparation.

For a correction covering several original invoices, create a separate customer credit and use Reviewed Credit Allocations in its UAE tab. Select each posted local original and enter its allocated net/VAT in the credit’s currency. The original number/date are preserved; company, customer, currency, category and process must match. External originals require authentic number/date and External Original Review Evidence, without a fabricated accounting invoice. Allocation totals must match the credit’s own net/VAT. Preparation protects the rows; XML contains separate original references, while allocation amounts stay in internal evidence. These links do not reconcile accounting balances. Volume discount handling remains unresolved.

If controlled batch validation stops a prepared document locally, its UAE tab shows Local Sending Review, reason and recording time. Use Validate UAE E-Invoice to inspect the preserved document. The document is excluded from subsequent automatic batch selections while other eligible invoices continue. This records no remote attempt or ASP rejection and does not modify the posted invoice or frozen evidence. Automatic correction/unblocking and complete operational recovery remain required work.

For scheduled controlled sending, accounting managers open UAE Provider Bindings, select the reviewed Sandbox binding and enable Automatic Controlled Dispatch. Support must configure a native Odoo cron worker and protected local API credentials. The default is off; production bindings cannot enable it. The native job checks eligible committed invoices every minute. Queuing on posting is a separate company option. API acceptance leaves fiscal/delivery confirmations pending. Missing scheduler configuration leaves the invoice queued; ask support to inspect native Scheduled Actions. Archiving prevents new submissions but retains reconciliation of existing attempts while scheduling stays enabled. Disabling scheduling also stops automatic lookup.

Fiscal Evidence Auditor users with separate native Data Export permission can download an issued revision’s Fiscal Evidence Package from UAE Audit. Its versioned ZIP preserves the snapshot/XML, attempts, original events and related billing/correction metadata with a hash inventory. Existing preserved conventional files are included without rendering. Corrupt originals and partial access are refused. See scenario25 in the PT/EN manuals. This audit export does not replace backups, retention/holds or a restored-database verification.

Accounting managers can open Invoicing → Configuration → UAE Document Deadlines to review an explicit basis, inclusive due date, reminder date, responsible user and requested fact for an invoice or credit note. Approve the reviewed basis before requesting an activity. Assess Deadline is read-only and uses the current UAE day; Schedule Internal Reminder creates one native To-Do when eligible. The hourly job checks approved records and isolates records requiring renewed owner/access review. API receipt and fiscal confirmations remain distinct. Completion of an activity does not reset reminder history. See scenario28 in the PT/EN manuals. These internal reminders do not determine legal timeliness or notify the FTA; full incident/calendar and escalation requirements remain open.

With native product-manager rights, use Customers → Products → New and open UAE Classification. A service requires a reviewed SAC and scheme version; goods require HS, and a reviewed mixed item requires both. No classification code or SAC version is supplied automatically. Single-variant template fields update that variant; multiple variants must be configured individually. Ambiguous template classification writes are refused. Select the actual product in invoice lines before preparation. Later product edits do not rewrite the prepared payload, XML or ledger. See scenario47 in the PT/EN manuals for the service journey and its controlled-provider limits.

For a reviewed Summary Invoice, either explicit Invoice Period Start or Invoice Period End owns the period as a unit. Neither is filled from accounting dates. If both are absent and native deferred line dates are installed, complete dated economic lines can supply the enclosing interval. These dates become protected fiscal sources after preparation. Other native deferral dates remain ordinary accounting data. Due dates and payment installments never supply missing period dates. See scenario63 in the PT/EN manuals for the Enterprise journey; Community supports explicit reviewed header dates.

Known issues / Roadmap

The integration is under development. The following release gates remain open:

  • Complete end-to-end acceptance for sending, authenticated signed-event ingress, receiving and self-billing, including scheduler authority and company isolation.
  • Qualify multi-currency, multiple credit references and advances across complete business flows; confirm UUID assignment and correction policies with the chosen ASP.
  • Complete operational recovery qualification, including bounded backoff, batch recovery and reconciliation after interruptions.
  • Qualify the remaining security, load, backup/restore (RPO/RTO), upgrade/rollback and PINT-AE ruleset transition scenarios on Odoo 20.0, including Enterprise coexistence.
  • Agree accredited ASP API contracts and replace the KMEE-controlled ClearTax and Suntech/TCA simulations with documented, provider-reviewed adapters. Validate authorized ASP sandbox/pilot flows and fiscal reporting before production use; controlled mock acceptance is not FTA confirmation.
  • Complete and review the English and Brazilian Portuguese user guides and site, plus the ten complete training videos by role with visible navigation, English narrative captions and no audio narration.
  • Complete hosted CI, reproducible release packaging and deployment runbooks, then prepare the contribution for community review and a proposed OCA umbrella repository.

Selected passing tests do not by themselves establish complete acceptance or production readiness. Public publication and deployment require their own readiness review.
